Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. 7-Day Free Trial

Auvii offers a 7-day free trial on new accounts to test AI chat widgets, product recommendation features, and knowledge ingestion before committing to a paid plan.

2. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time via your account dashboard. Upon cancellation, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current paid billing cycle.

Subscription fees are non-refundable except where required by applicable law or in cases of severe platform service interruptions confirmed by support.
