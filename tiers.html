How website crawling and the Sales Assistant work

Website crawling

A crawl is one request to scan and update your website's information. Each plan includes a set number of crawls and a maximum number of pages per crawl.

Free Trial1 crawlduring the trial
Basic1 / monthup to 25 pages
Standard5 / monthup to 50 pages
Premium10 / monthup to 100 pages
Your existing training data stays available after a crawl. Crawl limits only control when you can start a new website crawl.

Sales Assistant with product cards

The Sales Assistant can show your customers actual product cards in the chat. Each card includes:

Product name
Price

Short description of the product.

View Product
Example layout of a product card
  • Product image
  • Product name
  • Price
  • Short description
  • A clickable View Product button

The button takes the customer to the actual product page on your store.

The Sales Assistant is included in the Free Trial, Standard and Premium. It is not included in Basic.
